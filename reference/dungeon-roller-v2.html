Turn the sound on, then press Replay. Every sound is synthesized live by Tone.js from oscillators and filtered noise, with no audio files. The die, the wall, the torchlight and the shadows are now rendered by Three.js, while the gate, scroll and banner are the same HTML and SVG as before. The gold marks in the Sound lane are the cues: each one fires when the playhead crosses it during playback, so scrubbing stays silent.
